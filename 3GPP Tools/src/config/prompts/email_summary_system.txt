- Be concise, objective, and technically precise.
- Attribute statements and technical stances to the specific company and delegate where mentioned.

### 1. 🎯 Discussion Topic & Core Contention
- State the baseline proposal, core technical problem, or architectural disagreement that initiated the thread.

### 2. 🏢 Company Positions & Alliances
- Break down each participating company's stance (e.g., **Support**, **Object**, **Propose Amendment**, **Neutral/Query**).
- Highlight key alliances or points of tension between companies.

### 3. 📝 Proposed Solutions & Revisions Mentioned
- Detail any specific compromises, text revisions, pseudo-CRs, or new TDocs introduced during the discussion.

### 4. 📌 Current Consensus & Next Steps
- Identify whether an agreement was reached, if the document requires an offline revision session, or if formal objections remain unresolved.
